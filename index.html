Mission & Vision

Commitment To Medical Departments In Rendering Excellent Care, Research, And Education

Our Vision

To create a competent and socially responsible health care system by strengthening and sustaining medical knowledge, altruistic service and progressive research. To create highly competitive and skilled medical professionals.

Our Mission

To impart the best medical education to students through state-of-the-art teaching, training methodology, and creating opportunities for self-learning dovetailed with soft skills and ethical medical practice. To provide an affordable and quality healthcare delivery by means of updated technology of international standards and to innovate by providing collaborative research environment.

Life At Trichy SRM

Celebrating festivals enriches traditions and cultivates life skills.

Rasang 2025

A vibrant cultural festival where students showcased their artistic talents through music, dance, drama, and traditional performances, creating an energetic and memorable experience for the entire campus.

Margazhi Isai Thiruvizha

A celebration of classical music and cultural heritage, featuring soulful performances that highlighted the beauty of traditional arts and brought together students, faculty, and guests.

Navaratri

A colourful and spiritual celebration marked by devotional performances, traditional Golu display, and festive events that embraced cultural unity and joy.

Pongal Celebrations

A traditional harvest festival celebrated with enthusiasm, showcasing cultural activities, folk performances, and the joy of togetherness among students and staff.

Independence Day

A patriotic celebration honouring the spirit of the nation with a ceremonial flag hoisting, cultural events, and student performances reflecting unity and national pride.

Onam Celebrations

A festive occasion filled with traditional games, music, dance, and the iconic pookkalam designs, celebrating the cultural richness and harmony of the Onam festival.
