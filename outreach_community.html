Health Camps

Empowering communities through preventive healthcare and awareness programs

General OPD Medical Camps

A total of 34 schools were covered under the programme, including 19 Panchayat Union Middle Schools, 6 High Schools, and 9 Higher Secondary Schools. During the screening, 5,562 children were examined, and 347 students were referred for spectacles, comprising 173 boys and 174 girls.

Corporate Camps

Under Corporate Camps, a total of 8 companies/factories were covered, with 1,202 employees attending the screening programme. This included 1,004 males, 198 females, 9 individuals referred for further evaluation (Next), and 1 PET case. Among those screened, 84 employees were referred, comprising 69 males and 15 females.

Festival Camps

A total of 12 camps were conducted, with 2,555 individuals attending the programme, including 858 males, 1,589 females, 50 Next cases, and 60 PET cases, of whom 52 participants were referred for further management.

Residential Camps

Under Residential Camps, a total of 8 special schools were covered, with 814 students attending the programme, including 422 boys and 392 girls, of whom 177 students were referred for further evaluation and management.
