Diagnostic Laboratory & Imaging Services

Comprehensive NABL-accredited diagnostic laboratory and imaging services for accurate clinical evaluation


Clinical Pathology

Clinical Pathology focuses on the examination of blood, urine, and body fluids to help diagnose, monitor, and prevent diseases. Our laboratory is equipped with modern diagnostic instruments to ensure accurate and timely results.

  • Routine and specialized blood investigations
  • Urine, stool, and body fluid analysis
  • Hematology and coagulation testing
  • Automated analyzers for precise results
  • Qualified pathologists and lab technicians
  • Strict quality control and hygiene standards

Clinical Biochemistry

Clinical Biochemistry deals with the analysis of chemical processes in the body to assess organ function and metabolic disorders. Our biochemistry lab delivers reliable results using advanced equipment and standardized procedures.

  • Liver, kidney, and thyroid function tests
  • Blood sugar, lipid profile, and electrolyte analysis
  • Hormone and enzyme level testing
  • Fully automated biochemistry analyzers
  • Experienced biochemists and laboratory staff
  • Fast turnaround time with accurate reporting

Microbiology

Microbiology focuses on identifying infectious agents such as bacteria, viruses, and fungi to guide effective treatment. Our microbiology lab follows strict safety protocols to ensure reliable diagnostic support.

  • Bacterial, fungal, and viral testing
  • Culture and sensitivity analysis
  • Infection screening and pathogen identification
  • Advanced microscopes and incubation systems
  • Trained microbiologists and technical staff
  • Strict infection control and biosafety measures

Emergency & Critical Care Services

Round-the-clock emergency response with advanced life-support facilities

ICU

Our Intensive Care Unit (ICU) is designed to provide advanced, round-the-clock care for critically ill patients. The ICU is equipped with modern life-support systems, continuous monitoring, and a highly trained medical team to handle emergency and complex medical conditions.


  • 24/7 patient monitoring
  • Advanced medical equipment
  • Experienced doctors and nursing staff
  • Emergency response readiness
  • Safe and hygienic environment

PACU

The Post-Anesthesia Care Unit (PACU) is a specialized recovery area where patients are closely monitored after surgery and anesthesia. Our PACU ensures a safe, comfortable, and smooth recovery with continuous observation by trained medical professionals.


  • Continuous monitoring after anesthesia
  • Pain management and vital sign observation
  • Advanced recovery and monitoring equipment
  • Experienced anesthesiologists and nursing staff
  • Immediate response to post-surgical complications
