Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)

Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) is a dedicated body established in educational institutions to ensure continuous improvement in academic and administrative quality. It develops quality benchmarks, promotes effective teaching–learning processes, supports accreditation activities, and fosters a culture of excellence through systematic evaluation and stakeholder feedback.
